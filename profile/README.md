Real-time intelligence over SEC filings — every Form 4 insider trade, every 13F institutional holding, every Schedule 13D activist position, every 8-K material event — structured at the schema, surfaced within seconds of being filed, queryable from any AI assistant.

## What we ship

- **Live filing feed** — sub-60-second latency from SEC EDGAR to your dashboard
- **DEHY Score** — a reproducible 0–100 conviction signal on every insider trade
- **Insider Conviction Index** — a composite ticker score blending insider, institutional, and activist signals into one number
- **MCP server** — query the corpus from Claude, Cursor, or ChatGPT natively
